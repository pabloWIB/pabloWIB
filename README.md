## About Me

Anyone can build with AI now. The hard part is making it work for real customers and getting it to market.

I'm a full stack developer with 4+ years of experience and I run **[wib.labs](https://www.wib.digital/)**, a small software and marketing studio. Client repositories are private.

## What I Do

1. **Build it.** Web apps, internal tools, CRMs, websites and Android apps, or finish what Lovable, Bolt or v0 left half done.

3. **Put it in front of buyers.** SEO, Google Maps, landing pages with tracking, and Meta or Google ads once the offer sells.
